Score from your wrist.

Easy Score turns your Apple Watch into a simple scorekeeper for friendly matches. The screen is split into two colors: press your side to win the point. Games, sets and tie-breaks are counted for you.

  • Volleyball

  • Pétanque
  • Free counter

Highlights

  • Short press to score, Digital Crown to undo, distinct haptics for games, sets and change of ends.
  • Official rules built in: golden point, tie-break in 7 or 10, super tie-break, open match format or best of 1, 3 or 5.
  • Screen stays on during the match, optional workout saved to Health.
  • On iPhone: keep score without a watch, live tournament-style scoreboard, history, head-to-head records, share an image of the score, iCloud sync.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. One purchase, no subscription.

Available on the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch (iOS 17 and watchOS 10 or later).
